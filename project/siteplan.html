Pakistan Travel and Tourism Guide

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Pakistan Travel and Tourism Guide

This name was chosen because the website focuses on promoting Pakistan's tourism, culture, history, and natural beauty. It clearly describes the purpose of the website and its target audience.

Logo: A mountain and Pakistan flag inspired logo representing tourism, adventure, and national identity.

Site Purpose

The website will provide detailed information about Pakistan's most popular tourist destinations, historical landmarks, cultural attractions, travel tips, and natural wonders. Visitors will be able to explore destinations and learn useful travel information before planning a trip.

Scenarios

  • What are the best tourist destinations to visit in northern Pakistan?
  • What travel tips and safety information should I know before visiting Pakistan?

Color Schema

Primary Color: #006600 (Dark Green)

Used for headings, navigation, buttons, and important sections.

Secondary Color: #F5F5F5 (Light Gray)

Used for page backgrounds and content sections.

Typography

Poppins – Used for headings and titles.

Roboto – Used for body text and paragraphs.
